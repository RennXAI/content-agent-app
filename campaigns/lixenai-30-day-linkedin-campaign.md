## Design System Reference

### Canvas
- **Format:** 1080 × 1350 px (LinkedIn portrait feed)
- **Safe margin:** 64 px all sides
- **Spacing system:** 8-point grid
- **Primary corner radius:** 24 px
- **Secondary corner radius:** 16 px

### Color Palette
| Token | Hex |
|---|---|
| Midnight | #071426 |
| Deep Navy | #0C2138 |
| Lixen Blue | #1677FF |
| Electric Cyan | #25C7F4 |
| Signal Violet | #7559F2 |
| Growth Teal | #19C7A3 |
| Cloud White | #F5F8FC |
| White | #FFFFFF |
| Slate | #66758A |
| Charcoal | #172335 |
| Med-Spa Blush | #E9DCE7 |
| Alert Coral | #FF6B6B |

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Sora Bold or SemiBold
- **Body / Labels:** Inter Regular, Medium, or SemiBold
- **Category labels:** Inter SemiBold, All Caps, 11–13 px, letter-spacing 0.12em
- **Headline size:** 40–56 px depending on length
- **Body size:** 16–20 px
- **Max 3 headline lines; max 7–9 headline words**

### Logo Treatment
- White wordmark on dark backgrounds
- Midnight wordmark on light backgrounds
- Bottom-right placement, 64 px from corner
- Clear space = 1× logo height on all sides

### Visual Rules
- One dominant focal point per graphic
- Max three accent colors per graphic
- No robots, AI brains, fake money, or holographic clutter
- Thin 1 px borders in Lixen Blue (#1677FF) or Electric Cyan (#25C7F4)
- Shadows: 0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.25) on dark; 0 4px 16px rgba(7,20,38,0.08) on light
- Glass-panel effect: background rgba(255,255,255,0.06), border rgba(37,199,244,0.18)

### Recruitment Disclosure (required verbatim on all Partner recruitment posts)
> *Independent, performance-based Sales Partner opportunity. Not salaried employment. Income is not guaranteed.*
